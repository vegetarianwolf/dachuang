企业创新、产业升级与政府
引导基金的作用机制
邓晓兰，孙长鹏
（西安交通大学经济与金融学院，陕西西安710061）
［摘 要］在理论推导的基础上，实证测算了政府引导基金对企业创新以及企业创新对产业升级的推动作用，并利用案例
分析了政府引导基金的规模和结构问题。研究结果表明：政府引导基金具有通过促进企业创新推动产业升级的作用机制，且效
果显著；政府引导基金可集中作用于企业创新，由企业创新自发推动产业升级，从而摆脱财力限制与风控约束，兼顾创新驱动战
略与产业结构升级；政府引导基金发展到一定阶段后应转换规模和结构动能，这也为财政金融工具融合提供了参考。
［关键词］企业创新；产业升级；政府引导基金；产业结构；财政金融工具融合

Research on the Functional Mechanism among Enterprise Innovation, Industrial
Upgrading and Government Guiding Funds
DENG Xiao-lan, SUN Chang-peng
（SchoolofEconomicsandFinance,Xi'anJiaotongUniversity,Xi'an710061,China）
Abstract:Thispapertheoreticallydeducedandempiricallyestimatedthepositiveeffect of governmentguidingfunds on enterprise
innovation and the positive effect of enterprise innovation on industrial upgrading, then studied the size and structure of government
guiding funds through case study. The results show that, government guiding funds would obviously promote industrial upgrading
through enterprise innovation. And government guiding funds could mainly exert an influence on enterprise innovation, then enterprise
innovation may promote industrial upgrading spontaneously without the constraints from financial resources and risk management,
which would be conductive to innovation-driven strategy and industrial structure upgrading. At last, government guiding funds should
convert the scale and structural function when reaching a certain stage, which provides a reference for the integration of financial in－
struments.
