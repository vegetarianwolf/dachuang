Abstract : Abstract : Under the background of innovation-driven transformation and development ， this paper uses China's listed companies and macro data sets from 2007 to 2017 ， and constructs an empirical model to study the impact of fiscal “ technology expenditure on corporate innovation activities. This paper not only considers the relationship between fiscal technology expenditure—enterprise technology innovation” ， but also introduces the local government incentive structure into the above framework. The results show that fiscal technology expenditure is an important factor driving technological innovation activities of enterprises. Under the condition of distinguishing between enterprise attribute differences and regional differences ， fiscal technology expenditure has obvious heterogeneous innovation driving effect. In order to enhance the technological innovation momentum of enterprises ， it is necessary to consider whether the transmission mechanism of fiscal technology expenditure is smooth ， and should also make efforts to adjust the government incentive structure to enhance the scientific and effective of the fiscal technology expenditure.
