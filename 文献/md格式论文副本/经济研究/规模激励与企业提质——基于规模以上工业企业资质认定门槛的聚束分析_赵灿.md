基于全国税收调查数据，本文利用规上企业资质认定产生的政策断点，采用聚
束方法考察了规模激励冲击对企业规模及其绩效的影响。首先，检验了临规企业
针对资质认定门槛的规模响应行为，继而识别驱动企业聚束响应的内外部激励因
素，在此基础上剖析企业进行规模响应的具体途径。其次，进一步检验规模扩大激
励对企业提质增效的实际影响，并通过一系列稳健性检验确保结论可靠。最后，还
从地区税收征管力度视角出发，考察了企业可能因财务操纵行为而带来的效率损
失，并进一步检验了地方政府从企业“升规”中获得的潜在收益。实证结果表明，规
上企业资质认定门槛附近存在显著的规模响应行为，使得认定门槛右侧出现明显
的企业聚束现象。进一步研究发现，驱动企业聚束响应的主要因素包括地方政府
引导激励、资质信号传递激励以及企业自身发展需求，这些因素共同激励企业进行
规模扩张。规模扩张途径的分析结果表明，尽管临规企业确实通过实质性努力实
现规模扩大，但仍存在部分企业依赖财务操纵方式达到认定标准。进一步地，绩效
分析结果表明，规上企业资质认定门槛在激励企业扩大规模的同时也能促进企业
提质增效。然而，这一正向效应的实现依赖于企业的实质性努力，而通过财务操纵
方式实现的规模扩张并不能促进企业绩效的提升，进而带来财务操纵的损失。此
外，地方政府在临规企业“升规”过程中还可以获得税收增长与就业增加等收益。

本文的边际贡献体现在以下四个方面：其一，识别出了规上企业认定门槛附近
的规模响应现象，并首次量化评估了该认定门槛所产生的规模激励冲击及其对企业
提质增效的影响，为有效识别企业规模扩大与企业提质增效之间的因果关系提供了
新的研究思路。其二，解构了企业响应规模激励的途径，并识别出实质性努力对提
质增效的促进作用以及财务操纵行为所带来的绩效损失，有助于有的放矢地提出推
动企业提质增效以及规避“大而不强”的政策优化方案。其三，区别于已有研究更多
聚焦于高新技术企业、专精特新企业的资质认定信号发送作用，揭示了规上企业资
质认定同样具备信号发送作用，为理解资质信号发送的多元适用场景提供了新的视
角。其四，本文所采用的聚束方法是当前因果识别的前沿方法，能够较好地解决传

规模激励对企业提质增效的影响，进一步拓展了聚束方法的应用领域。

Scale Incentives and Firm Quality Improvement: Bunching
Analysis of the Qualification Threshold for Industrial
Firms above Designated Size
ZHAO Cana and LIU Qirena,b
(a: School of Economics and Statistics, Guangzhou University
b: Center for New Structural Economics, Guangzhou University)
Summary:How  to  promote  firms  to  grow  bigger  and  get  stronger  is  an  important  issue  in  building  a  mod‐
ernized industrial system. However, there is currently no consensus on this important issue.The qualification
recognition  policy  of  industrial  firms  above  designated  size  provides  a  valuable  opportunity  to  study  the
aforementioned  issue.  In  practice,  this  qualification  recognition  threshold  has  gradually  evolved  into  an  im‐
portant institutional discontinuity that incentivizes firms to expand their scale. Local governments often pro‐
vide  support  or  incentives  to  firms  approaching  the  recognition  threshold,  thereby  creating  external  incen‐
tives.  The  expansion  of  business  scale  required  to  obtain  the  qualification  helps  firms  improve  quality  and
efficiency.  Moreover,  the  act  of  obtaining  the  qualification  itself  has  a  signaling  function.  Therefore,  the
qualification  recognition  threshold  creates  clear  scale  incentives,  which  provides  a  suitable  policy  context
for  this  study  to  explore  how  to  achieve  a  win-win  situation  of  expanding  scale  and  improving  quality  and
efficiency.

However, the scale manipulation behavior of firms undermines the applicability of conventional causal
identification  methods,  and  the  regression  discontinuity  identification  strategy  is  rendered  inapplicable.
Therefore, this paper adopts a new causal identification method, namely the bunching method.Based on the
National Tax Survey Data, this paper adopts the bunching method to estimate the impact of scale incentives
on  firms’  scale  and  their  performance.  First,  this  paper  examines  the  scale  response  of  firms  approaching
the qualification recognition threshold, and then identifies the internal and external incentive factors driving
the bunching response. On this basis, it analyzes the specific channels through which firms achieve scale re‐
sponse.  Subsequently,  this  paper  further  examines  the  actual  impact  of  scale  incentives  on  firms’  improve‐
ments in quality and efficiency, and ensures the reliability of the conclusions through a series of robustness
checks.

Research  results  show  that  there  is  a  significant  scale  response  behavior  among  firms  approaching  the
qualification  recognition  threshold  for “industrial  firms  above  designated  size”.  Further  research  finds  that
the main factors driving the bunching response of firms stem from local governments’ guidance and incen‐
tives,  qualification  signaling  incentives,  and  firms’  own  development  needs.  The  analysis  of  scale  expan‐
sion  channels  shows  that  although  firms  approaching  the  threshold  do  expand  their  scale  through  substan‐
tive  efforts,  some  firms  still  rely  on  financial  manipulation  to  meet  the  recognition  standards.  Performance
analysis  indicates  that  while  the  qualification  recognition  threshold  incentivizes  firms  to  expand  their  scale,
it also promotes firms to improve quality and efficiency. However, the realization of this positive effect de‐
pends  on  the  substantive  efforts  of  firms.  The  contribution  of  this  paper  is  mainly  reflected  in  identifying
the  phenomenon  of  scale  manipulation  by  firms  approaching  the  identification  threshold  of  industrial  firms
above designated size, and quantitatively evaluating the impact of scale incentives generated by the recogni‐
tion threshold on firms’ performance. It is helpful to reveal the practical path of motivating firms to expand
scale and improve quality and efficiency, and provide policy references for building a modernized industrial
system.
